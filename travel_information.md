## Travel Information

[Lisbon](https://www.visitlisboa.com/en) is a welcoming and vibrant destination for conference attendees, offering a rich mix of history, culture, 
and modern convenience. The city is known for its scenic viewpoints, charming neighborhoods, excellent food, and 
easy public transport connections.

### Conference Location

NeSy 2026 will be hosted by the [Faculty of Sciences of the University of Lisbon](http://ciencias.ulisboa.pt).

[Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/9WDSNHHEoMjhcsUu5)

The campus is served by two metro stations (Campo Grande and Cidade Universitária), each within an 8-minute walking distance.
